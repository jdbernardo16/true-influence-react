🔍 Find Your Google Form Entry IDs

⚠️ Important: Each Google Form has unique entry IDs. You must extract the correct IDs from YOUR form.

Step 1: Open Your Google Form

Open your Private Client Inquiry Form in a browser:

https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScxaDXVN48iueQiOq9Q5kVcoHH-QI4V4Dz1STSpa-lVZgV1Bw/viewform

Step 2: View Page Source

Right-click anywhere on the form and select "View Page Source" (or press Ctrl+U / Cmd+U )

Step 3: Find Entry IDs

Press Ctrl+F / Cmd+F and search for entry.

You'll see entries like entry.1234567890

Copy each entry ID in the order of your questions (10 total)

Step 4: Update Your Code

Replace the entry IDs in src/components/PrivateClientInquiryForm.tsx with the ones you found:

fullName: "entry.1752943827" → YOUR_ID_1
email: "entry.1260464480" → YOUR_ID_2
phone: "entry.1997012052" → YOUR_ID_3
website: "entry.1965474626" → YOUR_ID_4
storyBlock: "entry.318364041" → YOUR_ID_5
visionGoal: "entry.735352643" → YOUR_ID_6
outcomeGoal: "entry.1836767856" → YOUR_ID_7
professionalPath: "entry.159948075" → YOUR_ID_8
investment: "entry.1391953150" → YOUR_ID_9
additionalInfo: "entry.2130249970" → YOUR_ID_10

Alternative: Use Browser Developer Tools

1. Right-click on a form field

2. Select "Inspect"

3. Look for name="entry.XXXXXXXX" in the HTML

4. The entry.XXXXXXXX is your entry ID
