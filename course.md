## Core Idea

Through five phases of training, leaders move from discovering their voice and standing confidently in their worth to building a message that shapes their brand, influences the people they lead, provides healed approaches to support trust and transformation in the people they lead, and leaves a meaningful legacy long after they are gone.

## Who is it for

**Accomplished women leaders:** CEOs, founders, executives, creators, and public figures — who have success, but still feel hesitation around fully owning their voice, story, and leadership.

**What they struggle with:** The method helps women move through these deeper internal barriers:

-   Lack of confidence speaking, and self-doubt
-   Imposter syndrome about belonging
-   Lack of clarity around the story that shaped their leadership
-   Feeling unsafe telling the truth of their real story
-   Lack of clarity of the unique value of their contribution (that can yield an effective succession plan and a lasting legacy to impact the future)

**End results by phase:**
They move from discovering their voice to building emotionally resonant influence, healed leadership frameworks, and a lasting legacy:

1.  **Phase 1: Tell Your Story:** Clarity of their message & story
2.  **Phase 2: Move the Room:** Develop your signature talk
3.  **Phase 3: Master Your Message:** Create thought leader-level resonance with your audience by standing in your unique value
4.  **Phase 4: Build Your Team:** Use healed leadership frameworks that create psychological safety and inclusion for a culture of trust, performance and profit.
5.  **Phase 5: Be Remembered:** Create a Legacy Blueprint standing in the value of your identity, capital and contribution that lasts beyond your lifetime. Includes impact thesis, succession plan and legacy letter.
